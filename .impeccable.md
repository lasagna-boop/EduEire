## Design Context

### Users
Primary audience is Irish applicants and current students. They browse quickly on mobile and desktop to discover useful study locations, then jump into relevant community discussions.

### Brand Personality
Premium, social, and energetic. Core tone is confident and polished, with community warmth.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial premium UI with social-network vibe. Keep brand anchors as green and orange accents, avoid generic dashboard flatness, and prioritize fast-scan hierarchy.

### Design Principles
- Optimize for "find a place fast, then open threads" workflow.
- Use green/orange as intentional focal accents, not everywhere.
- Keep surfaces clean but layered with depth and motion.
- Maintain accessibility and reduced-motion fallbacks.
